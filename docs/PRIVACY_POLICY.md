# Been There: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [date of publication]_

Been There is published by [Developer name]. This policy explains what the
app does with your information.

## The short version

Been There has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The developer does not
receive your location, your photos, your travel history or any other data
from the app.

## What the app stores on your device

- **Location.** With your permission, iOS tells the app when you move a
  significant distance or arrive at or leave a place. The app works out the
  country and city using a map database inside the app, and saves the result
  in your travel log on your device.
- **Photos.** With your permission, the app reads the date and location of
  your photos to rebuild past trips. It also shows your own photos inside
  your trips, in your Year in Travel, and in the On This Day widget, which
  keeps one resized copy of a photo on your device. The app never uploads or
  edits your photos.
- **Imports.** Google Timeline and flight files that you choose to import
  are read on your device.
- **Settings.** Your home country, day limits, planned trips, notes and
  preferences are saved on your device.

## What leaves your device

- **Apple Maps.** To name the places you visit and to draw maps, the app
  sends coordinates to Apple's maps and geocoding services. Apple handles
  these requests under its own privacy policy. The developer never sees
  them.
- **iCloud Photos.** If you open a photo that is stored in iCloud, iOS
  downloads it from your own iCloud account.
- **Sharing and exports.** Share cards, CSV and JSON exports and backup
  files leave the app only when you choose to share or save them.

## What the developer collects

Nothing. There is no developer server. The app contains no advertising,
analytics or tracking code, and it does not track you across other apps or
websites. If you have agreed to share analytics with app developers in your
iPhone settings, Apple may provide the developer with anonymous, aggregated
crash and usage statistics.

## Notifications

Reminders and recaps are created on your device. No push notification
service is used to send you content.

## Your choices

- You can turn location, photo and notification access off at any time in
  iOS Settings. The app keeps working with manual entries and imports.
- You can export your history, and you can erase it in Settings with
  "Delete all my data". That removes your travel log, notes, home country,
  day limits, planned trips, wishlist and the data shared with the widgets.
  Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.

## Children

Been There is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect
personal information from anyone.

## Not legal or tax advice

Day counts in the app are a planning guide based on your recorded days.
Check the official rules for your own situation.

## Map data credits

City and region names come from GeoNames (geonames.org), licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Country borders come from Natural Earth
and airport locations from OurAirports, both in the public domain.
