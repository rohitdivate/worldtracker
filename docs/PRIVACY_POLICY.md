# Been There — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 19, 2026_

Been There is built on one principle: **your travel history is yours.**

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Been There has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads,
and no third-party SDKs. We (the developer) receive no data from the app —
not your location, not your photos, not your usage, not even a crash report.

## What stays on your device

- **Location.** With your permission, iOS tells the app when you move a
  significant distance or arrive somewhere. The app converts that to a
  country/city using an offline atlas bundled inside the app — no network
  request is made. The resulting history is stored only on your device.
- **Photos.** With your permission, the app reads only the date and
  location metadata of your photos to reconstruct past trips. It never
  uploads, edits, copies, or even looks at the pictures themselves.
- **Imports.** Files you import (Google Timeline, flight CSVs) are parsed
  on-device and never transmitted.

## iCloud backup (optional, off by default)

If you turn on iCloud backup, your travel database syncs to **your own
private iCloud database**, run by Apple under your Apple Account. The
developer has no access to it. Turning the toggle off stops the sync.

## Sharing

Share cards (your year, months, trips, milestones) are images generated
on-device. They leave the app only when **you** choose to share them.

## Data deletion

Settings → "Delete all my data" erases everything the app has stored, on
the spot. Deleting the app does the same. There is nothing to delete
anywhere else, because nothing exists anywhere else.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will ship with the app update
that changes it, and this page will be updated.
